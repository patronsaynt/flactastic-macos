Home palette: sampled from the artist (example)

Up to four hues. Used for highlighted figures, number-one ranks, the genre chart, the Fidelidex and the glow behind the case. Home only. A grey or missing image keeps Home monochrome. UI for nerds sets the Accent to Artist (default), Mono or Custom.

The rule

A hue on screen is a quality tier, the Home palette, or album art. Nothing else. System red only on permanent-delete pills.

Typography

One family pushed to its extremes. Titles are heavy (800) with tight negative tracking, set like a record sleeve. Everything else is small and quiet. Numbers use monospaced digits.

Glow only from real light sources: the case's palette glow, the skip laser, the loader bloom. Floating windows (context menu) are the one control-level shadow.

The jewel case: the signature object

RackBox and RackDisc: a clear case with sheen and a 1px top highlight, a designed spine, and a disc printed from the cover. Thrown onto Home once per launch, then perfectly still. Always reuse it; never draw a new one.

Components

Tactile and plain. A pressed control dips to 0.96 and returns over 0.08s. Shown in both themes.

Player & Home

The player is one glass shape. The puck widens first (0.28s), then rises (0.26s) into the bar; closing is the exact reverse. Home is built from heavy section titles and 170pt cover shelves.

Motion

One house ease for arrivals. Big motion is saved for signature moments; after a showpiece, objects rest completely still. Every move has a Reduce Motion version.

The throw

Flies in from the far right, small and tumbling. Lands on the house ease, caught with a ~4×3pt jolt decaying over 0.16–0.2s. The disc is caught halfway out. Once per launch, then still.

CD skip

The case spins an extra turn, and a 2pt read laser sweeps the stage. The old content stutters (−12, 5, −20, −8) then tears off (x −140, skew −12°, blur 20). The new title springs in word by word.

Micro

Press 0.08–0.12s ease-out at 0.92–0.96 scale. Hover 0.15s. Selection 0.2s. List entrance 0.28s, 10pt rise, 15ms stagger capped at 0.25s. Theme flood pours from the click.

Page anatomy

Content hangs from one left edge. Page tops are tall, with one heavy title; lists are tighter.
